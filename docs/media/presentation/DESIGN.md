# OpenDuet showcase direction

Warm, quiet and editorial. Real product screenshots carry the story; captions
explain the outcome and the next decision. No invented product controls.

- Ground: `#f4f3ef`; paper: `#ffffff`; ink: `#141413`; muted ink: `#53565d`.
- Mark/accent: Claude's documented terracotta `#d97757`. Preserve OpenDuet's
  paired-stroke mark; do not use Anthropic's symbol or imply affiliation.
- Display: Newsreader Variable; body and labels: DM Sans Variable, matching the app.
- Canvas: 1440 × 960. Generous margins; 52–66 px titles, 22–28 px captions.
- Motion: 0.5-second opacity crossfades, subtle 16 px text entrances, no flash,
  zoomed UI, filters or distortion. Each scene remains readable for several seconds.
- Every product scene says “Scripted UI walkthrough · Sample data”. Video is
  silent; all meaning is available in visible captions and the showcase transcript.
- Story: delegate a task → review what leaves → repeat useful work → own the setup.
- CTA: github.com/ychampion/openduet, self-hosting and a real model required.

Color reference: [Anthropic's brand skill example](https://claude.com/resources/articles/how-to-create-skills-key-steps-limitations-and-examples).
The accent identifies the requested color; OpenDuet is an independent project.
